# Mount access correction

User identified that P01's side-mounted rail screw heads could not be inserted or reached. The user confirmed possession of M5 T-nuts and M3 plain/nylon locknuts; rail slot ends are not accessible. Existing M5 T-nuts remain the rail interface. No frame dimensions change.

Replace the enclosed side-mount cavities with top flanges on P01 and P02. Four vertical M5x16 screws use 3 mm TOTAL metal spacers/washers between heads and flange tops. Flange top Z63, head seat Z66, tip Z50: 5 mm penetration into rail top Z55, with1.2 mm clearance to the actual mesh web Z48.8. Direct access is from above. Use the user's suitable drop-in/roll-in M5 T-nuts; do not assume an ordinary M3 nut captures a slot.

P01 stop reinforcements become two M3x25 screws,0.5 mm washers and4 mm-high M3 locknuts in recessed hex pockets. Bolt tips stay2.5 mm inside stop end faces and extend2 mm past the nuts. Pivot through-bolt becomes M3x80 with two0.5 mm washers and an M3 locknut. Retain the structural8OD/5.2ID x48 mm metal sleeve; new printed P25/P26 centering inserts (OD5,ID3.4,length6) center the smaller bolt inside it. These inserts do not replace the metal sleeve.

Check actual nut height/head style and fit, tool access, hardware insertion, operating clearance, changed printable meshes and slice results.
