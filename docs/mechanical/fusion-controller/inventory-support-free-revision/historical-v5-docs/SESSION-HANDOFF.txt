3. DESIGN INTENT AND COMPONENTS

Train master controller with 3/5/7-gate replaceable cam discs and a spring-loaded
roller follower. Grip targets an EU locomotive palm grip: approximately 40 mm
wide, 60 mm long, pivot-to-crown 160 mm. This is a prototype ergonomic target,
not proof of matching every European locomotive's control or travel.

Coordinates: main shaft along X, at Y=0, Z=65 mm. Frame X +/-320, Y +/-95,
Z +/-55; long rails centered at Y +/-85, Z=45, 20 x 20 cross section.

P01/P02: left/right structural plates; P02 has sensor mounting rails.
P03/P04: PRINTED flanged journal bushes (not purchased bearings).
P05: PRINTED D-shaft; print horizontally on its D-flat, not standing upright.
P06: shaft retaining cap.
P07: integral lever rotor/cam carrier.
P08: cam alternatives with 3, 5 or 7 gates; 8 mm thick, three M3 fasteners.
     Seven-gate cam is normally visible; alternatives hidden in assembly.
P09/P10: split palm-grip halves.
P11: forked roller follower. P12: replaceable plain roller.
P13: spring reaction crossblock. P14: captive spring cup.
P15: 2 mm preload limit collar. P16: sliding replaceable RK097 mount.
P17: shaft-side clamp/input coupling hub.
P18: floating Oldham coupling disc, 4.2 mm grooves or alternate 4.1 mm grooves.
P19/P20: RK097 coupling clamp halves.
P21/P22: follower spacers. P23/P24: rotor thrust spacers.
P25/P26: follower pivot centering inserts.

There are 29 oriented STL files because cam and coupling fit alternatives are
included. Print ONE cam and install ONE P18 disc. Do not print the reference
frame, whole assembled model, or modeled purchased hardware.

Key fit assumptions:
- Shaft journal 12 mm, bush bore 12.3 mm; D-socket 0.2 mm diametral clearance,
  radial M3 clamp. Rotor total end float 0.3 mm.
- Cam stable positions span -50 to +50 degrees; structural hard stops +/-52.
- Cam service needs follower depressed approximately 5 degrees (~3 mm roller
  travel) and cam withdrawal 40 mm toward +X through the service window.
- Backing out the preload adjuster does not fully unload the spring. Restrain
  the cup/spring during service; follow the assembly guide.
- Candidate spring: free length 21 mm, OD <=8 mm, solid length <14 mm,
  approximately 10 N/mm. This is NOT a verified purchased spring selection.
  Nominal installed length 17 mm, operating/preload minimum about 15.2 mm,
  service-depressed about 16.3 mm. Verify actual force and coil-bind margins.
