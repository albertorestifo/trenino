# Direct-pot v6 physical-build corrections

The user has built direct-drive AIDesignedLever v6 and authorizes updating that design. This revision preserves the original v6 archive and reuses its sound printed parts; it does not modify the geared lineage. Mechanical and FDM AI specialist investigations precede edits, and final geometry is reviewed again before delivery.

## Chosen repair

Split P04 at Y=0 into radial half bushes, captured by the original P02 bearing seat and shaft. Preserve the existing main shaft, journals and shoulder. With rotor/spacers/cap removed, slide empty P02 from the left end across the central D shaft. Place bush halves radially on the right journal, then slide P02 rightward over their bodies until its outer face reaches the flange. Refit the rotor/spacers from the left. P02 cannot approach a fitted flanged bush from the right. This avoids reprinting a sound structural shaft.

Keep P18/P19 nominal coupling faces; move P16 and measured pot inward 3.2 mm. The body front becomes X65.95, fixed M7 bushing 58.95..65.95 and moving shaft 50.95..58.95. P19 grip 49.65..58.65 therefore overlaps 7.7 mm with 0.3 mm stationary-bushing clearance. Shorten P02 supports to 62.95 and move screws to 58.15; use accessible M3 hex nuts. Existing P16 slots allow adjustment; no forced axial preload on the pot.

P01/P13 inverted counterbores get two 0.2 mm bridge relief layers above unchanged screw seats. P04 halves print on their X-max flange faces, keeping the journal bore vertical and avoiding a semicircular tunnel roof. P08/P05 upward counterbores need no repair.

Replace accessible inserts in revised P02, P13 and P19 with stocked M3 hex nuts. P13 preload nut opens from the bottom and bears against an upper shoulder under spring load; guide nuts have lower shoulders. Block attachment nuts insert from the top. Standard M3 nut envelope assumed 5.5 AF x 2.4 thick; pocket 5.8 AF x 2.6. Confirm actual stock fit. Optional P05 and P07 revisions now also replace their accessible inserts with nuts. Only P09 retains inserts: a nut with the retained M3x 20 grip screws would leave about 1.4 mm or less backing at the socket. Its bores become 3.8 mm with 0.2 mm 45-degree mouth chamfers, responding to insufficient melt engagement. These are fit candidates, not measured compensation. Keep optional parts out of mandatory reprint list if existing inserts are secure.

Translate mechanism down 35 mm: pivot Z65 to 30, frame rail top remains 55, rail screw seats 64. Revise only P01/P02 mounting towers/flanges to reach the original rails. Retain direct-drive travel and cams (+/-52 mechanical, gates+/-50); this request matches height, not the geared transmission or extended cam system.

## Validation and delivery

Inspect native health, bush radial assembly and housing insertion, coupling overlap, spring preload/guide nut load paths, rail and mechanism motion clearance. Slice changed parts with unchanged default profiles and inspect critical bridges. Compare part geometry with baseline independently of assembly translation. Export mandatory and optional reprints separately with native archive, hardware delta and assembly notes. Physical strength, spring rate/solid height and actual print compensation remain unmeasured.

Final review refinements: P13 guide-nut closures and optional shaft joining-nut closures also receive sequential bridge relief. P13 rear wall extends 2 mm toY-76, improving the rear mount-nut ligament; guide ports continue through it. The outer guide nut retains 1.25 mm installed sidewall and is a lightly loaded restraint, not a rated structural joint.
