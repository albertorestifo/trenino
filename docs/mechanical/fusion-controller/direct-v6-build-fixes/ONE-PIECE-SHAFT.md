# One-piece shaft assessment

A one-piece shaft would remove the longitudinal joint, two joining screws and two alignment keys. It is technically feasible, but simply joining the current halves does not meet the support-free print intent.

The current main body is 16 mm diameter with a D-flat, while its running journals are 12 mm diameter and the integral retaining shoulder reaches 20 mm diameter. A flat under the central D section does not also provide a broad bed face under the smaller round journals. Flattening those journals enough for easy horizontal printing would remove useful bearing contact and can introduce position-dependent play. Printing vertically instead puts the long shaft's bending load across layers and creates widening shoulders that need different geometry or support.

A worthwhile one-piece alternative would use a continuous shaft with tapered non-bearing transitions and a separate end collar/drive hub. That can remove the shaft-half joining hardware, but needs a new retention interface and physical bending/fit testing. It would still have a separate collar, so it should be compared by total assembly steps rather than shaft body count alone.

For this repair package, keep the existing v6 shaft if it works. The split P04 bush and verified assembly sequence solve its insertion problem without a shaft reprint. The optional shaft pair is a nut-conversion upgrade, not a one-piece design. No unverified one-piece shaft is included in the print release.
