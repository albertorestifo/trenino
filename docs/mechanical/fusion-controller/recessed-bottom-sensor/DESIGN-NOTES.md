# Recessed lever with bottom sensor

The control panel's top is valuable for buttons and controls. The lever mechanism hangs between the existing rails from accessible top-rail tabs. The body is recessed; a perfectly flush outer surface is not required. Raised fixing pads and exposed screws are consistent with the requested locomotive-panel layout.

## Layout

- Existing frame: 640 × 190 × 110 mm; frame top Z55 mm, bottom Z−55 mm.
- Pivot: Z18 mm, 47 mm lower than the previous geared design, 37 mm below the rail tops.
- Potentiometer/pinion center: Z−27.15 mm, below the main shaft. The 48/24 tooth pair retains the 2:1 speed increase and opposite rotation.
- Large gear upper envelope: Z49.25 mm; 5.75 mm below the frame-top plane.
- Sensor pinion lower envelope: approximately Z−43.4 mm, inside the frame height.
- Mounting tabs: 9 mm thick on the upper rail tops, M5 screw seats at Z64, cap heads to Z69. The stocked M5 × 14 screw tips end at Z50, as in the previous top-mount arrangement. Actual T-nut engagement still requires a physical check.

## Panel concept

A reference 3 mm faceplate sits on the frame at Z55–58. Local edge cutouts clear the raised mounting tabs. A nominal 20 × 136 mm lever opening is included. This is a layout reference, not a finished sheet-metal panel or a printable part; panel fastening, button locations, legends, cable routing and any bezel are not yet detailed. Button bodies must also clear the mechanism underneath.

The grip and stem remain unchanged. Their swept envelope determines the opening. The main gear clamp is now above the shaft, outside the lower tooth-contact sector. The lower pinion keeps its clamp and open tool channel away from the top mesh. Do not use the upper-sensor version's indexing instructions or sideplates.

## Assembly and service

Assemble the mechanism between its hanging sideplates, then lower it into the frame and fasten from above. The lower sensor panel mounts in a shallow sideplate rebate, with its two screws outside the cam's service opening. To change a cam, remove the main gear and the lower sensor/pinion/panel assembly, then hold the follower retracted while withdrawing the cam. Recenter and recalibrate the potentiometer afterward.

No additional purchased hardware is intended: the existing four M5 × 14 screws, T-nuts, two panel M3 × 12 screws and insert count are retained. Printed changes are the two sideplates, the main gear clamp location and the lower sensor panel. Earlier frame, sensor travel, spring and hardware-fit assumptions still apply.

## Version separation

The coupling v6 and upper-sensor geared v3 designs remain preserved. This layout is a separate Fusion document, AIDesignedLever Recessed Bottom Sensor. Prior print packages do not contain these changes and must not be used as the complete print set for this layout.
