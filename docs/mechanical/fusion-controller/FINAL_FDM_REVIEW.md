# Final FDM specialist review — Fusion controller

Review scope: the NEW Fusion controller only, using build scripts 01–17, export manifest, supplied print orientations, mesh report, original slicer report and superseding normal-support re-slice report, assembly guide, and `fusion-overview.png`. The earlier Onshape review does not apply to this geometry. Joint script 17 is not assumed to have run, and this review does not replace motion validation.

## Disposition

The parts are generally designed to print in the supplied orientations on the P2S, with removable supports as listed. The rotor/lever now has a broad printing face, the printed shaft lies horizontally, the base is separate rail-mounted plates, and the rounded grip is split into printable halves. One **mandatory insert-retention correction** was found in P13 before release. Other items below are prototype assembly/fit checks, not reasons to claim the CAD cannot be printed.

### Blocker: P13 cam clearance cuts through two insert sites

The raised crossblock mounting holes nearest the cam are centered at y=-51, z=52, on both ends of P13. The cam-clearance cylinder is R51 centered at y=0, z=65. The distance from that hole center to the cut is:

`sqrt(51² + (65−52)²) − 51 = 1.63 mm`

The Ø4 pilot radius is 2 mm, so even the pilot breaks into the clearance cut. The installed CNC Kitchen insert is approximately Ø4.6; its 2.3 mm radius leaves **negative wall thickness** toward the cam. Heat-setting here cannot produce a fully enclosed retaining boss. Mesh watertightness does not detect this design problem.

Recommended local correction: move the matched P13 and P01/P02 mounting-hole pairs from y=-59/-51 to **y=-62/-54**, keeping z=52, and move the four screw models with them. The y=-54 insert has approximately 2.24 mm nominal material between its outside and the R51 cut; the y=-62 insert has 1.7 mm nominal material to the block's y=-66 outer edge. The 8 mm center spacing leaves 3.4 mm between installed inserts. Recheck rail-head/tool clearances, export and re-slice the affected three parts. Do not simply fill the hole without preserving the cam-clearance envelope.

## Evidence and release limits

- The mesh report records 26 single connected, closed, consistently wound meshes, all on Z=0 and within the P2S envelope. This includes three cam alternatives; one installed controller uses 24 of those printed solids.
- All original slice records report success. For the 11 supported parts, `slice-report-reslice.json` supersedes the tree-support runs with normal supports. That is important: the earlier tree runs reported lost-branch diagnostics even though they exited zero.
- The normal-support re-slices still carry printer-preset/tool diagnostics including `nozzle_volume_type` and `T65535`. Release geometry, not those CLI-generated G-code files. Slice with the user's installed P2S/filament profile and inspect support contact/removal in Bambu Studio.
- Slicer success is evidence that toolpaths can be generated; it does not prove support removal, running fits, insert retention, layer strength or fatigue life. Print the shaft/bush/coupling fits first and start with low spring load.
- The actual RK097 must be measured. The assumed Ø6 shaft/M7 mounting bushing, coupling engagement and usable travel remain hardware validation items. An Oldham disc's free motion must not be removed by axial bottoming of the hubs.
- FDM review does not certify native joint constraints or cam contact. The sampled motion and service-path checks must remain separately documented and repeated after any collision-relevant geometry changes.

**Review outcome:** release the supported PLA prototype geometry only after the P13/plate mounting-hole correction and affected mesh/slice checks. No other mandatory FDM CAD changes were identified in this review. The fork's thin sections, printed bearings, clamp fits, stop contact and real sensor fit remain explicit early bench-test items.

### Adjacent mounting-hole caveat

At the plate mount nearest the cam, the rail-head counterbore R5.3 centered at Z45 reaches Z50.3, while the M3 through-bore R1.7 centered at Z52 also reaches Z50.3. Their nominal voids are tangent where their Y ranges overlap (including the suggested Y=-54 mount). The actual screw envelopes may still clear, but the zero web is a stress concentration and needs inspection in the layer preview/first prototype. A local raised mounting boss with higher hole center would improve the durable version if it can be accommodated without changing the preload stack. This is distinct from the missing insert-retention wall in P13; no load or fatigue rating is claimed here.
