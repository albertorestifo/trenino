# Opposite-side assembly and service

This sequence accompanies the final CAD and oriented STL package. See Assembly-Guide.pdf for pictures and diagrams, FINAL-DESIGN.md for dimensions and VALIDATION.md for checks and physical commissioning limits. Print 32 pieces, selecting one of the four cam variants. Keep pre-gear v6 as the comparison baseline.

1. Print interface coupons/critical small parts first: journal bushes and shaft halves, insert pilot in the intended orientation, gear/shaft clamp and follower pivot. Check the actual pot shaft, spring solid height and supplier nut/washer before printing the large plates.

2. Heat-set inserts with parts separated and with clear iron access. Do not install inserts across the assembled shaft split. Let parts cool before loading the inserts. The follower's service insert is separate from its roller axle.

3. Join the main shaft halves around their loose alignment key using two M3x8 screws. Fit the two independent thrust spacers and rotor onto the central D section. Set the rotor clamp lightly; keep split-line faces fully closed.

4. Slide the left and right bushes onto their respective ends of the shaft. The right shaft has no enlarged integral end shoulder. Place the assembly between P01 and P02, then install the two crossbraces and eight M3x20 screws. Check rotation and axial movement before tightening the rotor clamp.

5. Fit each positive-location stop pad into its P01 socket and retain it with recessed M3x16. Turn the rotor gently to both stops: no pot or cam is fitted yet. Do not impact-test an unproven printed assembly.

6. Fit the keyed cam carrier from the left, its retaining cap/M3x8 and radial M3x10 clamp. Keep clearance to the bush flange. Attach the selected cam with three outward-facing M3x10 screws. Seven gates provide a central notch; eight gates have a central crest.

7. Assemble the roller follower using two 623ZZ bearings and the stocked M3x20 axle/nut. The printed pads bear on the inner races. Fit the captured split printed pivot, inner axial spacer, outer support with its integral compression posts. Secure the outer support with its two recessed M3x20 screws. The follower must move without being pinched.

8. Assemble spring cup, guide sleeves and M3x12 guide screws on the reaction block; retain the stocked 9x20 spring between cup and follower tail. Attach reaction block to P01 with two M3x20 screws. Start with the preload screw fully backed off to its documented position, not removed. Verify the actual spring never reaches coil bind through normal and service travel.

9. Install the pot in its removable panel with its supplied nut/washer. Fit pinion to the measured 6mm moving shaft. Set the lever and pot to their centers before tightening the clamps. Fit the main gear at the specified axial location; set mesh within45.0–45.5mm center distance, with free rotation and measurable clearance. Confirm the pot has sufficient travel for240° plus margin; otherwise restrict lever travel before use.

10. Mount on the top rails with four M5x14 screws and four stocked2020 T-nuts. Confirm nut orientation, thread engagement and clearance to the rail web. Route pot wires clear of every moving part.

## Cam service

Back preload fully off. Put the lever at its center reference. Move the follower to the absolute -4° service position and install the temporary M3x12 screw through the outer plate into its dedicated tail insert. Confirm it positively holds the follower before removing cam screws. Remove the three cam screws and draw the cam outward to the left; leave the carrier, shaft, gears, pot and bearing plates in place. Install the next cam, remove the service screw, and restore preload gradually. Never operate the lever with the service screw installed.

## Comparative commissioning

For v6 and this geared build record: assembly time, cam-change time, breakaway force, notch force spread, reversal dead travel, electrical noise/usable counts, and repeated-cycle wear. Use identical sampling/filtering. A2:1 gear ratio increases sensor angular travel; it does not guarantee twice the effective resolution or eliminate backlash.

Hardware for one geared build: 16 M3x20, 3 M3x16, 7 M3x12 (including temporary service screw), 4 M3x10, 3 M3x8, one plain M3 nut, 32 M3 inserts, four M5x14, four T-nuts, two 623ZZ bearings, one spring and one pot with its supplied nut/washer.

Both builds together require 26 M3x20 screws: use 20 from the kit and six from the separate stocked Torx pack at accessible crossbrace positions. Stock counts are estimated; physically recount and verify heads. No inventory has been consumed.
